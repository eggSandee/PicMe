Global Settings

Videos

iPhone (HEVC) videos are converted to H.264 in the background so every browser can play them. Larger clips are scaled down to the max size. Live Photo clips are the 2–3 second videos saved alongside Live Photos.

Gallery Mat

Color applies to solid frames only.

Default Metadata

One size for every slot on screen, set by the smallest. On the mat, pills sit in a caption under each photo and never cover it. Lower-priority pills hide when there's no room (never the date), and text too long for its pill scrolls.
